Universal InPage to Unicode & Unicode to InPage Urdu Converter

This free online converter supports both InPage → Unicode and Unicode → InPage (compatible with InPage 2000, InPage 2009, InPage 2012, and InPage 3) using Windows-1252 glyph encoding so your Urdu text pastes cleanly in Noori Nastaliq without question marks.

How to Paste Unicode Urdu Into InPage Without Question Marks?

Click Unicode → InPage above, paste your Unicode Urdu text into the left box, verify that your Windows keyboard layout in the bottom-right taskbar is set to ENG, click Copy Text, and press Ctrl + V inside your InPage text box.

How to Convert InPage Text to Unicode Urdu?

Select your Urdu text inside InPage, copy it with Ctrl + C, and paste it into the left box in InPage → Unicode mode. Your standard UTF-8 Urdu text appears immediately for WhatsApp, Facebook, MS Word, or websites.
