Why Does Urdu Appear Backwards & Disconnected in Photoshop?

Older versions of Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, and After Effects lack Right-to-Left (RTL) shaping engines, turning connected Urdu words into separate backwards letters (such as ا ر د و).

This tool reshapes each Urdu letter into its proper Arabic Presentation Form glyph and reverses line order so when you paste it into Photoshop and select a Nastaliq font, it renders properly connected.
